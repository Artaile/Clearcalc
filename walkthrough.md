# Walkthrough - Recent Updates

## 1. Amortization Schedule (EMI Calculator)
- **Feature**: Added a detailed month-by-month breakdown of loan repayments.
- **Details**:
    - Shows Principal, Interest, and Balance for each month.
    - Default view shows the first 7 months.
    - **"View Full Schedule"** toggle expands to show the entire tenure.
    - Includes a fade effect for better UX.

## 2. Banking Calculator Enhancements
- **PPF Calculator**:
    - Added a new tab for Public Provident Fund (PPF).
    - Calculates maturity amount based on annual contribution and 15-year tenure.
- **Inflation Adjustment**:
    - Added an optional "Inflation Rate" input.
    - Calculates the **Real Value** of returns (purchasing power) based on the expected inflation rate.
    - Helps users understand the true worth of their investment in the future.

## 3. General Updates
- **Task List**: Updated `task.md` to reflect progress on Banking and EMI features.
- **Design**: Maintained the dark-mode glassmorphism aesthetic across new components.
